Privacy Policy

This policy explains how THEWINNER (the "Platform"), operated by {{OPERATOR_NAME}} for our Discord server, handles personal data. We aim to follow the principles of Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU No. 27 Tahun 2022, "UU PDP"). We do not claim any certification.

Who is responsible

{{OPERATOR_NAME}} decides why and how your data is processed. Contact: {{SUPPORT_CONTACT}}.

Data we collect

  • From Discord (OAuth2, scopes identify and guilds.members.read): user ID, username, display name, avatar, and whether you are a member of our server. We never receive your password, email, messages or friends list. The Discord access token is used once during login and is not stored.
  • Verification record: your Discord ID, username and the date and time you were verified.
  • Whitelist record: Discord ID, a short staff note, the date added, and a setting saying whether the whitelist is required.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used in memory for rate limiting and may appear in our hosting provider's logs. We use no analytics and no advertising trackers.

Cookies

Two strictly necessary cookies: tw_state (10 minutes, protects the login) and tw_session (2 hours, keeps you logged in). Both are HttpOnly. They are not used for tracking.

Why we process it

To confirm you are a member of our server, record that you completed verification and accepted the rules, and apply our whitelist. Our basis is your consent when you log in and tick the rules box, and our legitimate interest in protecting the server.

How long we keep it

Verification and whitelist records are kept while you remain a member of the server, or until you ask us to delete them. Cookies expire as stated above. We do not delete records automatically, so please ask us if you want yours removed.

Who can access it

Our staff, our Discord bot (lookups only), and the team member whose computer stores the verification workbook. Access to the workbook service needs a secret key.

Your rights

Under UU PDP you may ask to see, correct, or delete your data, withdraw your consent, or object to processing, and you may complain to the competent authority. Write to {{SUPPORT_CONTACT}}.

Deletion requests

Send a request through Support. We confirm your identity through Discord, then remove your verification and whitelist records. Deleting your record means you will have to verify again to regain access that depends on it.

Third parties

Discord (login), our website hosting provider, and a secure tunnel provider that carries encrypted traffic between the website and the computer storing the workbook. These providers may process data outside Indonesia.

Security

HTTPS, HttpOnly signed cookies, CSRF protection, rate limiting, secrets kept out of the code, and an authenticated workbook service. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell affected people if a breach affects them, as the law requires.

Age

Use the Platform only if you are old enough to use Discord under Discord's own terms.

Changes

We update this page when our practices change and show the date at the top.
