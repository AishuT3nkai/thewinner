What this is, and what it keeps.

What the platform does

THEWINNER connects members of our Discord server to our own verification process. You log in with Discord, we confirm you are in the server, you accept the rules, and the result is saved in our verification record. It is a private system for this server, not a public verification service.

How the bot connection works

The website does not give your Discord login to anyone. After you verify, a record is written to our verification workbook. Our Discord bot can look up whether a Discord ID is verified through a private, authenticated API. Only the website's backend can create a verification. The browser never decides that you are verified.
