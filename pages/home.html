How it works

  1. Log in with Discord. You approve on discord.com. We never see your password.
  2. We check the server. Our backend confirms your account is a member.
  3. Accept the rules. One checkbox: server rules, Terms and Privacy Policy.
  4. Recorded. Your ID, handle and the time are saved. Then you go back to Discord.

What we read

Your Discord ID, username, display name, avatar, and whether you are in the server.

What we never do

Ask for your password, read your messages or email, post for you, or sell your data.
